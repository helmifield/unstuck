# UNSTUCK Product Specification

## Positioning

**UNSTUCK — Understand what's really going on before you make a move.**

UNSTUCK is not a breakup app, therapist, dating coach, or relationship fortune teller. It is a relationship intelligence product for understanding situations and deciding what to do next.

## Contexts

- Talking stage
- Dating
- Situationship / HTS
- Relationship
- Breaking up
- Someone from the past
- Something else

## Input model

Users can provide context at three levels:

1. **Tell** — describe the situation naturally.
2. **Answer** — answer targeted questions.
3. **Show** — optionally provide a screenshot or conversation.

Chat is never required.

## Core analysis

The engine can evaluate relevant signals including:

- Interest
- Effort
- Consistency
- Intent
- Clarity
- Reciprocity
- Compatibility
- Attachment
- Risk

Not every signal appears in every context.

## Core experience

`What's going on?` → context → tell/answer → initial read → curiosity → deeper context → deeper read → next move.

## Move-On

Move-On is one context, not the entire product. It uses an Unstuck Score and curiosity-driven breakdowns rather than treating a low score as failure.

## Chat analysis

Optional conversation analysis can answer:

- Are they into me?
- Are they serious?
- What does this mean?
- What should I reply?
- Any red flags?
- Could this be a scam?
- What's the dynamic?
- Am I being delusional?

Conclusions must be evidence-based and expressed with uncertainty when evidence is limited.

## Monetization

Value comes before account creation or payment.

Free experience should provide real value. Pro can unlock deeper analysis, history, unlimited checks, advanced decision tools, and other premium intelligence.

## Growth

- Shareable result stories inspired by Wrapped-style storytelling.
- Privacy-safe conversation result sharing.
- Annual `Your 2026 Dating Recap` style experience, opt-in.

## Design

Editorial, minimalist, Gen-Z, premium, warm ivory, black typography, restrained accent, large typography, generous whitespace, and minimal hand-drawn illustration. Avoid generic wellness, romance, AI, and SaaS aesthetics.
